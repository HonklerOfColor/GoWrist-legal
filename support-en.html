Supported cameras

GoPro HERO9 Black, HERO10 Black, HERO11 Black, HERO11 Black Mini, HERO12 Black, HERO13 Black and MAX 2 with current firmware. GoWrist requires watchOS 26 or later and no iPhone app.

How do I pair my camera?

In GoWrist, tap “Pair Camera”. On the GoPro: Preferences → Connections → Connect Device → GoPro Quik App. Keep the camera close to your watch. You only pair the camera once.

The camera is not found.

Check that the camera is in pairing mode (see above) and that Bluetooth is on on your watch (Control Center). Allow GoWrist to use Bluetooth on your watch in Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth.

“The camera no longer knows this watch.”

The camera has deleted the pairing. Remove it in GoWrist under Cameras (swipe left) and pair it again.

“Firmware is too old.”

Update the camera with the GoPro Quik app.

“May still be recording”

The connection dropped while recording. GoWrist never stops a recording by itself. Once the watch is connected again, stop the recording with the shutter button or Double Tap.

Putting the camera to sleep and waking it

The sleep button next to the shutter puts the camera to sleep to save its battery. The same button wakes it again without recording. The shutter wakes it and starts recording right away. GoWrist never wakes a sleeping camera by itself.

Action Button and Control Center

Add the “Toggle Recording” control to Control Center or the Smart Stack. On Apple Watch Ultra you can assign it to the Action Button in the Settings app.

Complication

Touch and hold the watch face, tap Edit and choose GoWrist’s “Camera Status” complication. It shows the last known state of the camera.

Is there a live preview?

No. GoPro offers live preview and media transfer only over Wi-Fi or USB, not over Bluetooth.

Contact

Please send questions, bugs or requests as an issue on GitHub.
