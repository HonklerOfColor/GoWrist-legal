Summary: GoWrist does not collect personal data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising. Everything the app stores stays on your Apple Watch.

What is stored locally on your Apple Watch

  • Paired cameras: the Bluetooth identifier your Apple Watch assigns to the camera, the camera's name (e.g. "GoPro 1234"), the camera model and when it was paired.
  • The last known camera status for the complication and the control (e.g. recording, battery level, camera name, time of the last update).
  • Your settings (e.g. after how many minutes to disconnect) and whether you put a camera to sleep.

This data does not leave your Apple Watch and is not sent to us or to anyone else. You delete it by removing a camera in the app or by deleting the app.

Bluetooth: GoWrist uses Bluetooth only to connect to and control your GoPro camera. The app searches for cameras only on the pairing screen and does not connect to other devices or to the internet.

No sharing: We do not share any data because we do not receive any. The app contains no third-party software for analytics, crash reporting or advertising.

Apple: Purchases, downloads and updates are subject to Apple's privacy policy. If you agree in your device settings, Apple provides developers with anonymized crash reports and usage statistics. This is controlled by Apple, not by GoWrist.

Children: GoWrist is not directed at children and does not collect data from them either.

Your rights: Since we do not process personal data, we hold no data about you. If you have questions, contact us via an issue on GitHub (link below).

Changes: If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date above.
